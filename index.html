Frequently Asked Questions

Which services do you offer?

Responsive websites, landing pages, website testing and bug fixing, improvements and maintenance, spreadsheet help, and AI workflow prototypes.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Websites are built to adjust to phone, tablet and desktop screens, and I check the layout at those sizes before delivery.

How do you test the work?

I review the layout on different screen sizes, check that menus, links and forms work, and fix problems I find before handing over.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Revisions are discussed and agreed before the work starts, so the scope stays clear for both sides.

What do you need to start a project?

A short description of your business, what you want the website or workflow to do, any text, logo or files you already have, and your preferred deadline.
